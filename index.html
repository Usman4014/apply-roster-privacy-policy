Privacy Policy – Apply Roster

Effective Date: November 9, 2026
Developer: Usman Tech Labs

1. Introduction

Usman Tech Labs ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Apply Roster handles information when you use our mobile application.

Apply Roster is a job application tracking application designed to help users organize job applications, track application progress, manage reminders, and monitor their job search activities.

2. Information We Store

Information you enter into the application may include:

  • Company names and job titles
  • Job locations and employment types
  • Job application URLs and salary information
  • Application dates, sources, and statuses
  • Personal notes
  • Reminder titles, descriptions, dates, and completion status
  • Application status history
  • Display name and application preferences

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information into application notes.

3. How We Store Your Information

Apply Roster uses local storage technologies, including SQLite and SharedPreferences, to store application information and preferences on your device.

The application is designed to operate locally and does not require an account to use its core features. We do not operate a cloud account or synchronization service for your job application data.

4. Notifications and Reminders

Apply Roster uses local notifications to remind users about important job application activities, including interviews, follow-ups, and other reminders.

Notification preferences are stored locally. You can manage notification permissions through your Android device settings.

5. Android Backup and Restore

Apply Roster does not explicitly disable Android's Auto Backup functionality. Depending on your Android device, Google backup settings, and system backup services, eligible application data may be included in Android backup and restore operations.

This may allow supported application data and preferences to be restored when setting up another Android device. Backup availability and behavior are controlled by Android and the device's backup settings.

6. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Apply Roster does not provide a cloud synchronization service for your job application information.

When you open a job application URL or another external link, it is opened using an external application or browser. Information you provide to that external website is subject to its own privacy policy.

Android system services may also process eligible data as described in the Android Backup and Restore section.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your application information is retained locally on your device until you delete it or remove the application data.

You can:

  • Delete individual job applications from the application.
  • Delete individual reminders from the application.
  • Manage notification permissions through Android settings.

Apply Roster currently does not provide a dedicated in-app option to delete all stored application data at once.

Uninstalling the application generally removes its locally stored data from the device. However, eligible data previously included in Android backups may remain in the backup service and could be restored according to your Android backup settings.

8. Data Security

Application data is stored using Android's local application storage mechanisms. We take reasonable steps to handle application information responsibly. However, no method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

Users should keep their devices protected with appropriate screen locks and device security settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Apply Roster is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a cloud-based account or registration system.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or privacy practices change. Updates will be published on this page along with a revised effective date.
